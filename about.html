About Stackly

Built by the villages we serve.

Fourteen years, six states, one belief: communities know their own solutions — our job is capital, craft and patience.

Our founding

It started with one dry well

In 2012, our founders spent a monsoon season in a village outside Kolar where the only well had run dry for the third year running. What began as a single borewell project turned into a promise: never build something a community can't run itself.

Today Stackly is a registered public trust working across four program areas, with 90% of field staff hired from the districts they serve.

How we've grown

2012

First borewell, Kolar district

A single well for 40 families becomes the model for everything after.

2015

Education program launches

Bridge schools open in three districts for out-of-school children.

2018

Mobile health camps begin

Partnership with regional hospitals brings monthly care to 40 villages.

2022

Livelihoods & cooperatives

Women-led weaving and farming cooperatives reach financial independence.

2026

128 villages, six states

Stackly's model is now studied and replicated by four partner NGOs.
