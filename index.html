Why we exist

Change that stays after we leave

Too much aid disappears the moment the funding cycle ends. Stackly builds differently: every well, classroom and clinic is designed, staffed and owned by the community it serves, so progress keeps compounding long after our teams move on.

For fourteen years we've measured success not by what we deliver, but by what keeps running without us.

Good to know

Common questions

Everything you might want to know before you give, volunteer or partner with us.

How much of my donation reaches the field?

96% goes directly to programs; the remainder covers audited administration and compliance.

Can I sponsor a specific village?

Yes — our partnerships team can match your contribution to a named village project with quarterly reports.

Is Stackly tax-exempt?

All Indian donations qualify for 80G tax exemption; international giving is supported via our FCRA account.

How do I volunteer on the ground?

Field placements open twice a year — apply through our Contact page and our team will reach out within a week.
