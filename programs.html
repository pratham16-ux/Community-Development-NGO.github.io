Our Programs

Four pillars, one community-owned model.

Every program is co-designed with local leaders, staffed by local hires, and built to be handed over.

Where every rupee goes

Programs built to be inherited, not maintained

We work in four areas that consistently determine whether a rural family can build a stable future: water, education, health and livelihood. Each program follows the same discipline — listen, co-build, hand over — so gains outlast any single grant cycle.

01 · Water & Sanitation

Clean water, close to home

Borewells, rainwater harvesting and household filtration, paired with hygiene education, cut waterborne illness by up to 70% within a year of installation.

  • 18 water systems built and community-managed
  • Village-run maintenance committees with trained technicians
  • Menstrual hygiene & sanitation facilities in every school we serve
Fund a Water Project
02 · Education

Learning centres for every child

Bridge schools bring out-of-school children back into mainstream education, while teacher training and scholarships keep them there through secondary school.

  • 6 community learning centres, 1,100+ children enrolled
  • Teacher training partnerships with 3 state education boards
  • 420 scholarships awarded to secondary & college students
Sponsor a Learner
03 · Health

Mobile clinics, real care

Monthly health camps bring doctors, maternal care and nutrition support to hamlets that are hours from the nearest hospital.

  • 40 villages covered by our mobile health network
  • Maternal & child health monitoring for 2,600+ mothers
  • Nutrition supplement program for children under five
Support a Health Camp
04 · Livelihoods

Income that stays in the village

Women-led cooperatives in weaving, dairy and farming give families a second income stream and a stake in local economic growth.

  • 34 self-help groups formed, 96% loan repayment rate
  • Farmer training in drought-resistant crop techniques
  • Market linkages connecting cooperatives to city retailers
Back a Cooperative
